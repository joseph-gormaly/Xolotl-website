02 / 14 The Market Flaw

The Collapse of "Cloud Sovereignty":
The US CLOUD Act Trap.

  • Extraterritorial Jurisdiction: Under the 2018 US CLOUD Act, domestic US providers must disclose data stored anywhere globally—rendering geographic hosting in Frankfurt or Montreal legally moot.
  • Centralized Key Escrow: Enterprise KMS solutions (AWS KMS, Azure Key Vault) retain master key control. A single subpoena served against the provider forces full plaintext disclosure.
  • The Ransomware Silo Trap: Pure on-premises air-gapped NAS/SAN setups disrupt distributed workflows and lack continuous, immutable offsite disaster recovery against modern encryptors.
  • The Institutional Crisis: Over $120B in enterprise data must comply with Schrems II, NIS2, and Swiss FADP, leaving CIOs trapped between unusable silos and legally compromised clouds.

05 / 14 Cryptographic Moat

Solving the FROST Non-Determinism Trap:
Pedersen DKG + Threshold ElGamal.

  • The Signature Nonce Problem: Standard FROST (RFC 9591) signatures produce randomized nonces per round, making deterministic derivation of static AES Key Encryption Keys (KEKs) impossible without key leakage.
  • Threshold ElGamal Decryption: Xolotl pairs Pedersen Distributed Key Generation with Chaum-Pedersen zero-knowledge proofs over Ed25519 for deterministic KEK unwrapping.
  • Session Coordinate Blinding: Every unlock request blinds public points ($R' = R^\beta$). Intercepted partial decryptions are mathematically worthless in any future session.
  • Audited Foundation: Built on battle-tested frost-core and curve25519-dalek with strict ZeroizeOnDrop RAM protection.
