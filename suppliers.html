Sovereign Privacy Framework

EU GDPR · ePrivacy Directive · Swiss FADP · Canadian PIPEDA Adequacy

1. Zero Tracking Telemetry

This web domain (xolotl.ca) employs zero Google Analytics, Meta Pixels, or third-party marketing beacons. Navigation cookies are strictly limited to local session dismissal preferences.

2. Cryptographic Zero-Knowledge Mirroring

Client data synchronized to our Montreal storage cluster is encrypted using customer-controlled AES-256-GCM symmetric keys derived from multi-custodian threshold consensus. The cooperative holds zero decryptable shares.
